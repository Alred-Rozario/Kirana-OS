EARLY OBSERVATIONS FROM 12 RESPONSES
41.7% of shops still bill by hand and another 33.3% use a standalone billing machine — no respondent yet uses an integrated shop-management system.
66.7% track inventory in a paper register; only one respondent (8.3%) uses dedicated inventory software.
75% restock only weekly, which raises the risk of stock-outs going unnoticed between visits.
83.3% use no software or app at all; the 16.7% who do rely on generic billing apps (e.g. Vyapar), not an inventory + billing + udhaar system.

SECTION 06
Problem Statement
From current situation to the core problem and the need
7
CURRENT SITUATION  —  evidenced by 12 pilot responses
Manual records
66.7% track inventory in a register or notebook
Scattered billing
41.7% still bill by hand; 33.3% use a standalone machine
No software adoption
83.3% use no software / app to run the shop
Stock uncertainty
75% restock only weekly — stock-outs found late
Expiry risk
Unsold perishable and confectionery stock
Udhaar tracking
Credit kept informally, no formal ledger
CORE PROBLEM
Small general, kirana and confectionery stores have no simple, integrated way to manage inventory, billing, expiry and customer credit together — and existing software is either billing-only or built for larger retail.
NEED
A practical, affordable and easy-to-use system combining inventory, billing, expiry tracking, supplier records and an udhaar ledger — usable by owners with limited exposure to software.
Every item in the current-situation row corresponds to a question already in the form (Sections 2 and 3); relative severity will be confirmed as more responses come in.
